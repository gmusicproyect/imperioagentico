# Constructor de Funnel de Ventas

## Cuándo Usar Este Skill

- Usuario tiene tráfico pero sin conversión
- Usuario quiere estructura clara de venta (awareness → decision)
- Usuario lanza nuevo producto y necesita full funnel
- Usuario dice "cómo hago mi primer venta"

## Principio Fundamental

UN FUNNEL EXITOSO NO ES 50/50 MAGICAL. ES 90% TARGETING CORRECTO (PERSONA CORRECTA) + 10% COPYWRITING BUENO.

## Estructura del Funnel

### ETAPA 1: AWARENESS (Tráfico)
- Landing page
- Offer: Lead magnet
- Métrica: Conversión a email > 10%

### ETAPA 2: CONSIDERATION (Email sequence)
- Email educativo (3-7 emails)
- Offer: Webinar/demo/trial
- Métrica: Click-through > 15%

### ETAPA 3: DECISION (Sales conversation)
- Call o demo
- Offer: Core product
- Métrica: Close rate > 20%

### ETAPA 4: POST-SALE (Onboarding)
- Welcome sequence
- Offer: Upsells/referral
- Métrica: Retention > 80%

## Workflow

### Fase 1: Define tu Producto/Precio

```
PRODUCTO BRIEF
==============
What you're selling: [Nombre]
Price: $[X]
Customer: [Persona]
Main benefit: [Outcome promise]
Time to value: [Cuánto hasta primer resultado]
```

### Fase 2: Build Landing Page (Awareness)

```
LANDING PAGE ELEMENTS
=====================

HEADLINE
--------
"[Specific outcome] for [persona] who [problem]"
Ej: "Build your agency to 7-figures without the chaos"

SUBHEADLINE
-----------
Adds urgency or mechanism
"In just 90 days"

BENEFITS (3-5)
--------------
✓ Benefit 1 (outcome)
✓ Benefit 2 (mechanism)
✓ Benefit 3 (proof)

OBJECTION HANDLING
------------------
"How is this different from [competitor]?"
"I don't have time"
"How much does it cost?"

CTA SECTION
-----------
"Download [leadmagnet] to get started"
Button: "Get [leadmagnet]"

FORM
----
Name + Email ONLY (no more)
```

### Fase 3: Email Sequence (Consideration)

**EMAIL 1 (Immediate)**
- Welcome, deliver leadmagnet
- Confirm you got email

**EMAIL 2 (Day 1)**
- Educate: How to use the lead magnet
- No sell yet

**EMAIL 3 (Day 3)**
- Story: How you solved this for self/customer
- Introduce problem deeper

**EMAIL 4 (Day 5)**
- Mechanism: How the solution works
- Introduce what you sell (high level)

**EMAIL 5 (Day 7)**
- Objection handling: "But is it worth it?"
- Social proof / testimonial

**EMAIL 6 (Day 10)**
- Introduce specific offer (call, demo, trial)
- Limited time incentive

**EMAIL 7 (Day 14)**
- Last chance / final CTA
- If not interested, unsubscribe link

### Fase 4: Sales Conversation (Decision)

```
CALL SCRIPT
===========
Duration: 30 mins

Agenda:
- Opener: Build rapport (2 min)
- Discover: Ask about their situation (10 min)
- Present: Show how you'd solve it (8 min)
- Close: Invite to next step (5 min)
- Logistics: Confirm timing/payment (5 min)

Offer: "Done-for-you audit" $500 or "3-month retainer" $3000
Close with specific next step: "See you Thursday at 2pm"
```

### Fase 5: Optimization (Repeat)

```
METRICS & TARGETS
=================

Landing page CRR: 15%+
(If lower: rewrite headline or improve targeting)

Email sequence open rate: 25%+
(If lower: improve subject lines)

Email CTR: 5%+
(If lower: improve email copy/CTA)

Call show rate: 75%+
(If lower: send calendar link + reminder)

Sales close rate: 25%+
(If lower: better qualification or offer design)

OPTIMIZATION ORDER
==================
1. Fix your targeting first (wrong person = low conversion no matter what)
2. Then improve headline (right person, wrong message = doesn't convert)
3. Then improve funnel copy (same person/message, weak execution)
4. Then optimize offer (right people/message, wrong price point)
```

## Ejemplo Completo

### Funnel: Coaching para Freelancers (Course-based)

```
FUNNEL STRUCTURE
================

AWARENESS
---------
Channel: Google ("freelance business coach")
Landing page: "90-Day Freelance Growth Blueprint"
Offer: Lead magnet ("Pricing Checklist")
Conversion goal: 15%

CONSIDERATION
-------------
Email 1: Welcome + deliver checklist
Email 2: "The #1 pricing mistake freelancers make"
Email 3: Story of freelancer who raised rates 40%
Email 4: Intro to what we teach ("systems thinking for freelancers")
Email 5: Testimonial from past student
Email 6: Webinar invitation ("Free masterclass: Escape hourly billing")
Email 7: Last chance to register

Conversión goal: 5% join webinar

DECISION
--------
Webinar (60 min)
- 30 min teaching (establish credibility)
- 20 min case study (proof it works)
- 10 min close to course offer ($1997, 3-month cohort)
- 10 min Q&A

Conversión goal: 10% buy during webinar

POST-SALE
---------
Email 1: Confirmation + first lesson access
Email 2 (Week 2): Encourage completion + community join
Email 3 (Week 4): Check-in + highlight success stories
Email 4 (Week 8): Upsell (1:1 sessions, accountability partner matching)
Email 5 (Week 12): Graduation + referral program intro

Retention goal: 80% complete course

NUMBERS (100 visitors)
======================
Visitors: 100
Optin (lead magnet): 15 (15%)
Email sequence completion: 12
Webinar registration: 1 (8%)
Webinar attendees: 0.75 (if 75% show rate)
Course buyers: 0.075 (10% close rate) = roughly 1 sale per 100 visitors
```

## Recuperación

- **Landing page converts < 5%**: Targeting es malo O headline es débil. Test nueva headline primero.
- **Emails have < 20% open**: Subject lines son débiles. A/B test con tema de línea diferente.
- **Nobody shows up a calls**: Calendar link está difícil de usar. Mejora UX o usa Calendly.
- **Calls no cierren**: Qualifying pobre o offer equivocado. Pregunta en call: "Cuán serio eres de 1-10?"

## Restricciones

- **NUNCA sales sin warming** — el frío se vuelve expensive
- **NUNCA generic headlines** — debe ser específico para persona
- **NUNCA funnel sin test** — pequeño test primero, escala después
- **NUNCA múltiples CTAs** — confunde a la gente. Un CTA por email.
