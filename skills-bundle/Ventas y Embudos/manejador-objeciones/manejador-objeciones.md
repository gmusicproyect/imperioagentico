# Manejador de Objeciones

## Cuándo Usar Este Skill

- Vendedor enfrenta objeciones recurrentes
- Usuario no sabe cómo responder "No"
- Usuario pierde deals a las mismas objeciones
- Usuario dice "cómo manejo [specific objection]"

## Principio Fundamental

LA OBJECIÓN NO ES RECHAZO. ES SEÑAL DE INTERÉS. Si no hay objeción, probablemente no les importa lo suficiente para considerar.

## Framework: FEEL-FELT-FOUND

```
Prospect: "Your price is too high"

Response:
"I totally understand. [A LOT] of people feel the same way.
Other clients FELT the exact same before they joined.
But here's what they FOUND: [specific ROI that justified price]"

Example:
"I totally understand. A lot of freelancers feel like $3,000 is expensive
for coaching. Other freelancers felt the exact same before they joined.
But here's what they found: they raised their rates $30/hour within month 1,
which paid for the entire program back in 4 months."
```

## Objeciones Comunes + Respuestas

### OBJECIÓN 1: "Precio es demasiado alto"

**Reframe (usa metrics):**
"The question isn't the price. It's the value. If I could show you how
to make back [X price] in 60 days, would that change things?"

**Si dicen sí:** Presente ROI específico
"Here's how: [mechanism]. On average, our clients see [$ or result] in [timeframe]."

**Alternativa:** Offer payment plan
"We can break it into 3 payments of $[X] instead. Does that help?"

### OBJECIÓN 2: "Necesito pensarlo"

**Red flag:** Probablemente significa "no" educado. Presiona un poco más.

**Respuesta:**
"I appreciate that. Let me ask—what specifically do you need to think about?
Is it the price, the timing, or whether this is the right fit?"

**Si dice "no sé":** Es débil interés. Move on.
**Si dice "timing":** Agenda call específico. "Let's chat again next Thursday at 2pm."
**Si dice "price":** Usa Feel-Felt-Found.

### OBJECIÓN 3: "Tenemos un vendor ya"

**Respuesta:**
"Got it. How's that working for you? What would be different if you could [solve specific pain]?"

**Si admite problema con actual:**
"Exactly. That's why a lot of our clients switched. Want to see how we do it differently?"

**Si dicen "funciona bien":**
"Great. Well, keep our info. If that changes, we're here."
(No insistas. La próxima vez volverán.)

### OBJECIÓN 4: "No tengo presupuesto"

**Real vs. Excusa:**
- Real: "We froze spend till Q3"
- Excusa: "Don't have budget right now"

**Si es real:**
"Got it. When does Q3 budget open up? Let's schedule time to revisit then."

**Si es excusa:**
"I understand. Let me ask—if this could save you [specific value], would you find the budget?"

**Si dicen sí:** Presenta case for cost savings
"Here's what typically happens: [mechanism saves money]. Would that be enough to justify it?"

### OBJECIÓN 5: "Necesito hablar con mi jefe/equipo"

**Normal. Maneja bien:**

"Of course. Who should we loop in? And what do you think they'll care about most?"

**Listen.** Si dicen "price", prepara angle de ROI. Si dicen "fit", prepara técnico angle.

**Schedule llamada múltiple:** "Let's get [person] on a call Thursday. You fill them in, then I'll answer any questions."

### OBJECIÓN 6: "Hace falta feature X"

**No subestimes:**
"Good question. How critical is that to solving your problem? Could you work around it?"

**Si crítica:** Ofertas alternatival. "Actually, here's how you could achieve [outcome] without it..."

**Si no crítica:** "Got it. We have it on roadmap. In the meantime, could you start with [Y]?"

## Template de Respuesta Propia

Para cada objeción común TU producto enfrenta:

```
OBJECIÓN: [State the objection]

FEEL-FELT-FOUND:
"I totally understand. [Empathy statement].
[Similar person] felt the same way.
But here's what they found: [specific result/benefit]"

ALTERNATIVE:
If Feel-Felt-Found doesn't land, try:
[Alternative reframe or offer]
```

---

## Recuperación

- **Prospect says "no" múltiples veces**: Accept it. Don't wear them down.
- **No sé si es objeción real**: Ask. "Is this a real concern or should we just table this?"
- **Misma objeción de 50% prospects**: Probablemente es tu offer. Repiensa precio/messaging.
- **Manejo mal una objeción en vivo**: "Actually, let me think about that and get back to you." No improvises.

## Restricciones

- **NUNCA argumente** — escucha primero
- **NUNCA hagas objeción más grande** — dale validez y mueve on
- **NUNCA des descuento ciego** — descuenta valor, no precio
- **NUNCA asumas la objeción** — pregunta qué importa realmente
