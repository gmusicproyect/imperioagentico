# Guión de Discovery Call

## Cuándo Usar Este Skill

- Usuario tiene un producto/servicio B2B y necesita un proceso de venta consultivo
- Usuario dice "¿cómo hago mi primer call con un prospecto?"
- Usuario quiere un guión que califique AL MISMO TIEMPO que descubre
- Usuario quiere demostrar expertise durante el descubrimiento, no solo hacer preguntas

## Principio Fundamental

UNA BUENA LLAMADA DE DESCUBRIMIENTO TIENE 3 METAS: CALIFICAR, DESCUBRIR, CERRAR UNA PRÓXIMA ACCIÓN. Nunca entreles sin saber si es un fit viable.

## Estructura del Guión

**Duración:** 30 minutos
**Objetivo:** Descubrir si hay fit, identificar siguiente paso, cerrar acción específica

### FASE 1: APERTURA (2 min)

```
"Hi [Name], thanks for jumping on. Before we dive in, I want to set expectations
for our time. I'm going to ask you a bunch of questions to understand what you're
working on, and then we'll figure out if there's a fit. Sound good?

The goal isn't to pitch you today — it's to understand if we can actually help.
Does that work?"
```

**Por qué:** Reduce presión, establece que esto es mutuo.

### FASE 2: CONTEXTO (3-4 min)

Pregunta:

1. "Walk me through your current role. What do you actually do day-to-day?"
   *Por qué: Entiende el contexto. Escucha para dolor específico.*

2. "What's on your plate right now? What keeps you up at night about [your area]?"
   *Por qué: Identifica urgencia. La urgencia = dinero dispuesto.*

3. "When you think about [problem area], what's the biggest frustration?"
   *Por qué: Refina el dolor. Escucha la emoción.*

### FASE 3: INVESTIGACIÓN (8-10 min)

Pregunta:

4. "How are you currently solving this? Walk me through your process."
   *Por qué: Entiende el statu quo. Hay miedo al cambio.*

5. "What's not working about your current approach?"
   *Por qué: Descubre las grietas. Aquí es donde entra tu solución.*

6. "What have you already tried to fix this?"
   *Por qué: Entiende su viaje. Valida si están serios.*

7. "If you could wave a magic wand, what would change?"
   *Por qué: Obtén su visión del éxito. Aquí es donde encajas.*

8. "What would fixing this be worth to you? (In time, money, peace of mind)"
   *Por qué: Ahora sabes si pueden comprometerse con dinero.*

### FASE 4: MOSTRAR EXPERTISE (3-4 min)

No hagas pitch. En su lugar, vuelve a formar lo que dijeron:

```
"So if I'm hearing you right, the real issue is [reframe their pain point].
Most companies we talk to are in a similar boat.

What we typically see is [insight basado en tu experiencia].
Have you run into that?"
```

**Por qué:** Demostraste que escuchaste Y que has visto esto antes. Autoridad + empatía.

### FASE 5: CALIFICACIÓN (2-3 min)

Pregunta crítica:

9. "Is this something you're looking to solve in the next 90 days, or are you exploring longer-term?"
   *Por qué: Califica urgencia. No quieres perseguir exploradores de 12 meses.*

10. "If we could help, who else needs to be involved in the decision?"
    *Por qué: Entiende la estructura de decisión. Evita sorpresas después.*

11. "What does the process look like if you decide to move forward?"
    *Por qué: Entiende políticas de compra. Algunos necesitan presupuesto, comités, evaluaciones.*

### FASE 6: SIGUIENTE ACCIÓN (1-2 min)

Si encaja:

```
"Based on what you've shared, I think there's a real fit here.
Here's what I'm thinking we should do:

[Option A: Demo / presupuesto $10K+]
"Let's schedule a 30-min demo where I show you how we'd approach this specifically for you."

[Option B: Quick pilot / presupuesto $1K-10K]
"Let's start with a 2-week pilot where you see the impact directly."

[Option C: Call with team / si hay múltiples decisores]
"Let's loop in [other person]. Then we can chat next steps."

What makes sense?"
```

Si NO encaja:

```
"Based on what you've shared, I don't think we're a fit right now.
But here's what I'd recommend [general advice].
If things change, keep us on your radar. Can I send you something that might help?"
```

## Ejemplos Completos

### Ejemplo 1: SaaS CRM para Agencias

```
APERTURA (2 min)
================
"Hi Sarah, thanks for hopping on. Quick agenda: I want to understand what you're
working on, and then we'll figure out if there's a fit. My goal isn't to pitch you
today — it's to see if we can actually help. Cool?"

CONTEXTO (3 min)
=================
"So walk me through your current role as Operations Manager. What does your day look like?"
→ [Listen for: Tech they use, pain points about managing teams, time spent on admin]

"What's on your plate right now that's frustrating?"
→ [Listen for: Urgency. If she's swamped, she's motivated.]

"When you think about client management, what's your biggest headache?"
→ [Listen for: Emotional language: "chaotic," "manual," "frustrated."]

INVESTIGACIÓN (8 min)
=======================
"How are you currently managing clients and projects?"
→ [Listen for: Spreadsheets, email, no system = big pain]

"What doesn't work about that setup?"
→ [Listen for: Time lost, things fall through cracks, team confusion]

"Have you tried other tools before?"
→ [Listen for: Yes = they understand value, No = might be hesitant about change]

"If you could redesign how you manage clients, what would change?"
→ [Listen for: Integration, visibility, automation, reporting]

"What would fixing this be worth? In terms of time saved or money made?"
→ [Listen for: "$50K in recovered revenue" = strong motivation]

EXPERTISE (3 min)
==================
"So it sounds like the core issue is that client info is scattered across
email, spreadsheets, and conversations. Most agencies we talk to are stuck there.

What typically happens is team members duplicate work, things slip through cracks,
and you can't see the whole picture. Does that ring true?"

CALIFICACIÓN (2 min)
====================
"Is this something you're looking to solve in the next quarter?"
→ [If yes: warm. If "exploring": cool them down.]

"Who else would need to sign off on a tool like this? Just you, or does your CEO
or CFO need a say?"
→ [Multiple decision makers = longer sales cycle]

"If we could show you how this works, what does your process look like? Do you do
a trial, get a demo, etc.?"

SIGUIENTE ACCIÓN
=================
"Based on what you've shared, I think there's a real fit. Here's what I'm thinking:

We do a 30-minute demo where I show you exactly how we'd set your agency up.
I'll show you a client account template, how reporting works, the whole flow.

After, you'll have a sense of whether this could work for you. Sound good?

How does next Thursday at 2pm look for you?"
```

### Ejemplo 2: Coaching para Freelancers

```
APERTURA (1 min)
================
"Hey Marcus, thanks for getting on. Quick heads-up: I'm going to ask you
a bunch of stuff about your freelance business, and then we'll see if
coaching makes sense. Cool?"

CONTEXTO (3 min)
=================
"Walk me through what you do. What kind of freelance work?"
→ [Listen for: rate, type of clients, how long he's been doing it]

"What's frustrating you right now about your business?"
→ [Listen for: "stuck," "too busy," "not making enough," "burnout"]

INVESTIGACIÓN (8 min)
======================
"Tell me more about that. What's the specific frustration there?"
→ [Press deeper. Get emotional.]

"How are you currently handling [growth / pricing / client management]?"
→ [Understand their current approach.]

"What would change if you could fix this?"
→ [Understand their ideal state.]

"On a scale of 1-10, how serious are you about fixing this in the next 3 months?"
→ [Qualify motivation. 7+ = real.]

"What's held you back so far from fixing this on your own?"
→ [Understand objections. Maybe needs accountability, guidance, community.]

EXPERTISE (3 min)
==================
"Here's what I see most freelancers stuck with: They're trading time for money,
so raising rates feels risky. But without raising rates, there's no way to make
more without burning out.

Have you hit that wall?"

CALIFICACIÓN (2 min)
====================
"Is this something you actually want to work on in the next 90 days?"
→ [No = not ready yet.]

"What would need to be true for you to commit to coaching?"
→ [Gets them to articulate investment.]

SIGUIENTE ACCIÓN
=================
"Here's what I'm thinking: We start with a 90-day coaching program.
You're in a cohort with 5-10 other freelancers, we meet weekly, and
I give you the frameworks and accountability to actually make this shift.

Most people end up raising rates $20-40/hour and cutting hours by 30%.
Sound interesting?"

[If yes: "Here's the investment and next steps..."]
[If hesitant: "What would make you more confident?"]
```

## Recuperación y Troubleshooting

- **Prospecto no contesta las preguntas**: Reformula. "I'm asking because..."
- **Conversación va en tangentes**: "That's interesting. Let me circle back to..."
- **No hay urgencia**: Cambia tack. "Sounds like this isn't urgent right now. Is that right?"
- **Prospecto es demasiado tímido**: Usa "feel/felt/found": "I know how you feel. Others have felt the same. Here's what we found..."

## Restricciones

- **NUNCA hagas pitch antes del descubrimiento** — rompe la confianza
- **NUNCA digas "no somos fit" sin preguntar primero** — deja eso para ellos
- **SIEMPRE cierra una acción específica** — "next Thursday, 2pm" no "let's talk soon"
- **SIEMPRE escucha más de lo que hablas** — si hablaste más del 20% del tiempo, fue una pitch
