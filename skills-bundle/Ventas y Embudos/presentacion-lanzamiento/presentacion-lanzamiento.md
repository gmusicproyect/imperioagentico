# Creador de Pitch Deck

## Cuándo Usar Este Skill

- Fundador necesita presentar a VCs
- Usuario presenta a prospectos grandes
- Usuario necesita estrutura narrativa clara para lanzamientos

## Estructura Estándar (12 slides)

1. **Portada** — Nombre empresa, logo, your name
2. **Problema** — What pain point exists
3. **Solución** — How you solve it
4. **Mercado** — TAM/SAM/SOM
5. **Producto** — Demo o visual
6. **Tracción** — Clientes, usuarios, revenue (si existen)
7. **Modelo de ingresos** — Cómo ganas dinero
8. **Competencia** — Panorama competitivo + posicionamiento
9. **Equipo** — Quiénes son (brevemente)
10. **Financiación Ask** — Cuánto necesitas, cómo lo usas
11. **Proyecciones** — Crecimiento esperado
12. **Cierre** — CTA ("Let's talk", contacto)

---

## Cada Slide Explicado

**SLIDE 1: PORTADA**
- Company name (large)
- One-liner tagline
- Your name + title
- Date

**SLIDE 2: PROBLEMA**
- Describe pain point específico
- Cuantifica: "X% de [target] enfrenta this"
- Show emotion: How does this feel?

**SLIDE 3: SOLUCIÓN**
- High-level: "We solve this by..."
- Benefit statement (not features)
- Why it's different

**SLIDE 4: MERCADO**
- Total Addressable Market: $X billion
- Why we can win: positioning
- Customer acquisition plan (1 sentence)

**SLIDE 5: PRODUCTO**
- Demo video o screenshot
- Core features (3 bullets max)
- Current status (beta, launch, scaling)

**SLIDE 6: TRACCIÓN**
- # customers / revenue / growth rate
- Testimonial (quote)
- Retention rate

**SLIDE 7: MODELO INGRESOS**
- Price point
- Revenue per customer
- Growth unit economics (CAC, LTV)

**SLIDE 8: COMPETENCIA**
- Competitive matrix
- Your position
- Why you win

**SLIDE 9: EQUIPO**
- Names, titles (2-3 key people)
- Relevant background (1 line each)
- Why this team can win

**SLIDE 10: FINANCIACIÓN**
- How much you're raising: "$X million"
- Use of funds (breakdown: product 40%, sales 30%, ops 30%)
- Timeline to next milestone

**SLIDE 11: PROYECCIONES**
- Revenue projections (12-36 months)
- Customer growth projections
- Show path to profitability

**SLIDE 12: CIERRE**
- "We're raising $X"
- "Let's build the future of [category]"
- Your email / contact info

---

## Narrativa Flow

```
OPENER (Slide 1-2):
"Imagine this... [paint problem picture]
This happens to [X] people every year"

AHA (Slide 3-4):
"That's why we built [product]
We're in a $XB market"

PROOF (Slide 5-7):
"We're already serving [Y] customers
They're paying us $Z/month"

OPPORTUNITY (Slide 8-11):
"Here's why we win
Here's how we'll scale
We're raising $X to get there"

CLOSE (Slide 12):
"Join us"
```

---

## Restricciones

- **NUNCA + de 12 slides** — brevedad = respeto
- **NUNCA por debajo de tamaño 16 font** — debe leerse
- **NUNCA sin traction** — si tienes usuarios/revenue, muéstralo
- **NUNCA genérico** — específico > bonito
