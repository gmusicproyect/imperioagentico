⚖️ LEGAL Y CUMPLIMIENTO
==================================================

Ignorar lo legal es una ilusión que sale cara. Ya sea contratos, protección intelectual, cumplimiento regulatorio o privacidad de datos, estas decisiones tempranas definen tu riesgo futuro. Esta categoría te da las plantillas, políticas y marcos legales que necesitas para operar correctamente sin pagar asesor legal por todo. Aprenderás qué documentos son críticos, cómo proteger tu propiedad intelectual, qué normativas aplican a tu negocio, y cómo estructurar acuerdos que protejan ambas partes. La mejor defensa legal es la prevención documentada.

Para Quién Es
- Fundadores que necesitan estructura legal básica
- Directores de cumplimiento (compliance)
- Equipos de contabilidad y finanzas
- Cualquier empresa que maneja datos de clientes o empleados

Los 30 Skills de Esta Categoría:

1. Acuerdo Asociacion
Drafts business partnership agreements with roles, profit sharing, decision-making, and exit provisions. Use when forming a business partnership or joint venture.

2. Acuerdo Cliente
Redacta acuerdos de servicio al cliente con alcance, entregables, plazos, límites de revisión y términos de pago. Usa cuando crees contratos para compromisos con clientes.

3. Acuerdo Contratista Independiente
Creates independent contractor agreements with scope, payment, IP ownership, and non-compete clauses. Use when hiring freelancers or contractors for your business.

4. Acuerdo Licencia
Drafts content and product licensing agreements with usage rights, royalty structures, exclusivity terms, and territory definitions. Use this skill when a creator wants to license their intellectual property, photos, courses, templates, music, or digital products to another party.

5. Acuerdo No Competencia
Creates non-compete agreements with reasonable scope, duration, geographic limits, and enforceability notes. Use when protecting business interests from competition.

6. Acuerdo Procesamiento Datos
Drafts data processing agreements for GDPR compliance with processing details and security measures. Use when you process personal data on behalf of another business.

7. Acuerdo Retenedor
Drafts retainer agreement templates with scope, hours, rollover policies, and termination terms for service providers.

8. Acuerdo Saas
Drafts SaaS subscription agreements with service levels, data ownership, and termination provisions. Use when creating terms for a software-as-a-service product.

9. Acuerdo Subcontratista
Drafts subcontractor agreements with scope delegation, payment terms, and quality requirements for service businesses.

10. Auditoria Propiedad Intelectual
Audits IP assets with trademark, copyright, trade secret, and patent inventory and protection recommendations. Use when assessing and organizing your business IP portfolio.

11. Aviso Copyright
Creates copyright notices and policies for digital content with DMCA procedures and fair use guidelines. Use when protecting original content and establishing copyright policies.

12. Carta Intencion
Creates letters of intent for business transactions with key terms, conditions, and timeline. Use when formalizing preliminary agreement on a business deal.

13. Cese Y Desista
Redacta cartas de cese y desista para infracción de PI, difamación o violaciones de contrato. Usa cuando necesites demandar formalmente a alguien que detenga actividad dañina.

14. Generador Descargos
Generates legal disclaimers for websites, products, and services with liability limitations and warranty exclusions. Use when adding disclaimers to your business properties.

15. Liberacion Exencion
Drafts liability waivers and release forms for events, services, and activities. Use when you need participants to acknowledge and accept risks.

16. Lista Cumplimiento
Creates industry-specific regulatory compliance checklists with documentation requirements and audit preparation. Use when ensuring your business meets regulatory obligations.

17. Lista Cumplimiento Gdpr
Creates GDPR compliance checklists with data mapping, consent mechanisms, and breach response procedures. Use when ensuring your business complies with GDPR requirements.

18. Plantilla Nda
Generates mutual and unilateral NDA templates with customizable confidentiality terms, exclusions, and duration. Use this skill when a user needs a non-disclosure agreement for sharing sensitive information with potential partners, contractors, investors, or collaborators.

19. Politica Accesibilidad
Redacta políticas de accesibilidad web con compromisos de cumplimiento WCAG y procedimientos de acomodación. Usa cuando crees una declaración de accesibilidad para tu sitio web.

20. Politica Cookies
Writes cookie consent policies with category descriptions, opt-out mechanisms, and GDPR/CCPA compliance. Use when creating or updating your website's cookie policy.

21. Politica Devolucion
Writes clear, customer-friendly return and refund policies for e-commerce stores, digital products, and service businesses with plain-language clauses and platform-specific formatting. Use when a user needs a return policy for their online store, wants to update their refund terms, or needs policies for digital products, subscriptions, or services.

22. Politica Envios
Writes clear shipping and delivery policies with rate tables, timelines, and international terms. Use this skill when a user needs a shipping policy for their e-commerce store, wants to document delivery timelines, or needs professional shipping and returns documentation for their website.

23. Politica Gastos
Creates corporate expense policies with spending limits, approval workflows, and reimbursement procedures. Use when establishing rules for business spending and reimbursements.

24. Politica Lugar Trabajo
Creates workplace policies covering remote work, equipment, communication, and professional conduct. Use when establishing rules and expectations for your team.

25. Politica Privacidad
Writes GDPR and CCPA-compliant privacy policies for websites, apps, and online businesses with plain-language annotations explaining each section. Use when a user is launching a website, setting up e-commerce, collecting customer data, or needs to update an outdated privacy policy.

26. Politica Terminos Pago
Creates payment terms and collections policies with net terms, late fee structures, and escalation procedures. Use when establishing how and when clients must pay.

27. Redactor Contratos
Drafts service agreements, freelance contracts, and partnership agreements with standard clauses and plain-language annotations explaining each section. Use when a user needs a professional contract template, wants to formalize a client relationship, or needs a starting point for legal review.

28. Solicitud Marca Registrada
Guides trademark application preparation with class selection, specimen requirements, and filing checklists. Use when preparing to file a trademark with the USPTO.

29. Terminos Servicio
Creates terms of service documents for websites, SaaS products, and digital platforms with plain-language annotations. Use when a user is launching a website, app, or online service and needs legal terms governing usage, liability, and user conduct.

30. Terminos Uso App
Creates terms of use specifically for mobile apps with license grants, restrictions, and app store compliance. Use when launching or updating a mobile application.
