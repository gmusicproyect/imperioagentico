# Secuencia de Bienvenida

## Cuándo Usar Este Skill

Usa este skill cuando necesites:
- Nuevos suscriptores
- Setup iniciales
- Primera impresión crítica

---

## Principio FUNDAMENTAL

LOS PRIMEROS 5 EMAILS DETERMINAN SI ALGUIEN SE QUEDA O SE VA — HAZLOS CONTAR.

---

## Structure (5 Emails, 10 Días)

**Email 1** (Inmediato): Bienvenida + quick win
**Email 2** (Día 1): Cuál es siguiente
**Email 3** (Día 3): Value drop
**Email 4** (Día 7): Community/conexión
**Email 5** (Día 10): Oferta

---

## Email 1: Welcome

```
Subject: Welcome, [Name]!

Hi [Name],

Welcome to [community/list].

You're now part of [X] people who [benefit].

Start here: [One thing to do]

Questions? Reply to this email.

Excited to have you,
— Team
```

---

## Email 2: Setup

```
Subject: Next step: [setup action]

Hi [Name],

Now that you're here, let's set you up.

Here's what to do first:
1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

Takes 5 minutes.

Video guide: [link]
```

---

## Email 3: Value

```
Subject: [Valuable resource]

Hi [Name],

As promised, here's [resource].

Use it for [benefit].

Enjoy,
— Team
```

---

## Email 4: Community

```
Subject: Meet the community

Hi [Name],

Here are other people on this journey:

[3 example testimonials]

You're not alone.

Our community: [link]
```

---

## Email 5: Offer

```
Subject: Welcome offer — [X]% off

Hi [Name],

Since you're new, special offer:

[X]% off [offer].

Code: WELCOME

Valid for 5 days.

[Shop now]
```

---

## Anti-Patrones

- **Immediate upsell** — establece valor primero
- **Sin quick win** — hacer alguien sienta fracaso early
- **Demasiados largo** — 5 max, no 10
- **Genérico** — personalize

---

## Métricas

- Click-through: 30-40%+
- Open rate: 40-60%
- Unsubscribe: <2%
