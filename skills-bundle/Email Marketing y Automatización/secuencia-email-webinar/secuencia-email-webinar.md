# Secuencia de Email de Webinar

## Cuándo Usar Este Skill

Usa este skill cuando necesites:
- Promover webinar
- Recordatorios pre-webinar
- Seguimiento post-webinar
- Conversión de asistentes

---

## Principio FUNDAMENTAL

EL OBJETIVO DEL WEBINAR NO ES INFORMACIÓN — ES CONVERSIÓN. TODO EL EMAIL DEBE MOVER A ESO.

---

## Timeline

**Semana 4:** Email 1 - Teaser
**Semana 2:** Email 2 - Invitación
**Semana 1:** Email 3 - Early bird
**Día anterior:** Email 4 - Reminder
**Día del:** Email 5 - Join now
**Día siguiente:** Email 6 - Replay
**Día 3:** Email 7 - Follow-up
**Día 5:** Email 8 - Offer/close

---

## Email Ejemplos

### Email 1 (Teaser)
```
Subject: Something is coming...

Hi [Name],

We're hosting a webinar on [topic].

[Benefit statement]

Details coming soon.
```

### Email 2 (Invitación)
```
Subject: You're invited to [webinar]

Hi [Name],

Join us [date] at [time].

We'll cover [3 topics].

This is for you if [target].

[Register now]
```

### Email 6 (Replay)
```
Subject: Missed the webinar? Watch the replay

Hi [Name],

You missed out on live chat, but you can watch:

[Replay link]

Share with your team: [link]

— Team
```

### Email 8 (Offer)
```
Subject: Implement what you learned

Hi [Name],

Ready to [transform/improve]?

Let's get started:

[Product/service offer]

Use code [WEBINAR] for 20% off.

[Buy now]
```

---

## Conversión Focus

- Teaser: Interest
- Invitation: Signup
- Reminder: Attendance
- Replay: Engagement
- Offer: Sale

---

## Anti-Patrones

- **Demasiadas invitaciones** — 3 máximo
- **Sin incentivo de urgencia** — "cuando quieras" no funciona
- **Replay sin offer** — leave dinero en tabla
- **Complicado registrarse** — máximo email + nombre

---

## Métrica

- Registration rate: 5-10%
- Attendance: 30-50% de registrados
- Replay views: 20-30% de registrados
- Sales: 1-3% de asistentes
