# Email Prueba a Pagado

## Cuándo Usar Este Skill

Usa este skill cuando necesites:
- Convertir trial users
- Reducir trial cancellations
- Mostrar valor antes de pedir pago

---

## Principio FUNDAMENTAL

DURANTE PRUEBA, DEBES PROBAR VALOR — DESPUÉS, PEDIR PAGO ES FÁCIL.

---

## Timeline

**Día 1:** Bienvenida + primer paso
**Día 3:** Primer win/resultado
**Día 7:** "Prueba a mitad" + incentivo
**Día 10:** Objeción address
**Día 12:** "2 días left" urgencia
**Día 14:** "Hoy expira" + conversión
**Post-14:** Re-engagement para re-trial

---

## Email Ejemplos

### Email 1 (Día 1)
```
Welcome to [product] free trial.

Next step: [Setup]

Video guide: [link]

Need help? [support]
```

### Email 3 (Día 3 - First Win)
```
You already [accomplished].

That's [metric] ahead of [baseline].

Keep using [feature] to [result].

Explore [next feature]
```

### Email 7 (Día 7 - Mid-trial)
```
You're halfway through your free trial.

See how [product] has [result].

Upgrade now and lock in $99/month (normally $199).

[Upgrade]
```

### Email 12 (Día 12 - Last Chance)
```
Your trial ends in 2 days.

Your progress:
- [Achievement 1]
- [Achievement 2]

Upgrade to keep going:
[Upgrade]

Or extend trial: [link]
```

---

## Métricas por Email

**Email 1:** Completion de setup
**Email 3:** Feature usage
**Email 7:** Trial -> paid conversion
**Email 12:** Last push conversion

---

## Incentivos para Convertir

- Discount first month (30% off)
- Locked pricing (founder rate)
- Bonus features (free tier)
- Extended trial (extra 7 days)

---

## Anti-Patrones

- **Esperar hasta final para vender** — debería estar a mitad de prueba
- **Sin prueba de valor** — mostrar achievements
- **Sin incentivo** — "just pay" no funciona
- **Demasiada presión** — gentle urging mejor

---

## Métrica de Éxito

- Objetivo: 5-15% trial -> paid conversion
- Good: 15%+
- Excellent: 20%+

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Onboarding fuerte (Email 1)

- [ ] Incentivo atractivo
