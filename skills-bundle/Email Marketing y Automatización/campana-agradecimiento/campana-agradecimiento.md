# Campaña de Agradecimiento

## Cuándo Usar Este Skill

Usa este skill cuando necesites:
- Post-compra immediate
- Construir relación
- Set tone para customer lifecycle

---

## Principio FUNDAMENTAL

UNA COMPRA NO ES VENTA FINAL — ES INICIO DE RELACIÓN. AGRADECE PRIMERO, VENDE DESPUÉS.

---

## Secuencia

**Email 1:** (Inmediato)
- Agradecimiento genuino
- Confirmación de orden
- Próximos pasos

**Email 2:** (24 horas)
- Shipping confirmation
- Resource útil
- Pequeño delight

**Email 3:** (3-5 días)
- Producto llegó?
- How-to guide
- Support info

---

## Email 1: Confirmación

```
Subject: Thank you for your order, [Name]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for choosing us.

Your order [#12345] has been confirmed.

What's next:
- Your package ships within 24 hours
- You'll get a tracking link
- Arrives by [date]

Questions? Reply to this email.

We're thrilled to have you.

— Team
```

---

## Email 2: Delight

```
Subject: A little something for you

Hi [Name],

Your order is on its way!

As a thank you, here's a 10% discount code for your next order: THANK10

It's good for 30 days.

— Team
```

---

## Email 3: Support

```
Subject: Got your package? Here's how to get the most from it

Hi [Name],

Your [product] should have arrived.

Here's what to do first:
1. [Setup step]
2. [Usage step]
3. [Optimization]

Video guide: [link]

Questions? Reply here or [support link].

— Team
```

---

## Tono

- Warm, grateful
- No hard sell
- Helpful, not pitchy
- Personal, not corporate

---

## Anti-Patrones

- **Immediate upsell** — wait at least 3 days
- **Generic copy** — personalize con nombre
- **Sin próximos pasos** — dejar cliente sin claridad
- **Sin support info** — customer necesita help

---

## Métrica

- Objetivo: 100% order confirmation sent
- 90%+ delivered successfully
- Setup email increases product usage 30%

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Email 1: Confirmation automático
- [ ] Email 2: Delight/bonus
- [ ] Email 3: Support + how-to
- [ ] Personalization (nombre)
